About Kenang-Kenangan Si Mbah

Bringing Tradition Home

Kenang-Kenangan Si Mbah is the official merchandise store of Pawon Mbah Gito — a tribute to the rich culinary and cultural heritage of Java. Every product is crafted with the same warmth, authenticity, and love that defines our traditional kitchen.

From T-shirts that tell stories to tumblers that keep your drinks warm, each piece is designed to bring a piece of Javanese tradition into your everyday life.
